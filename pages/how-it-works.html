How CivicFix Works

One Complaint. One Smart Decision. One Better City.

Understand how CivicFix transforms a complaint into real government action in less than two minutes.

Estimated Reading Time : 2 Minutes
01

Citizen Reports an Issue

The citizen reports a civic issue by uploading a photo, writing a description, selecting the category and capturing the exact GPS location of the problem.

02

AI Priority Analysis

CivicFix analyzes the complaint. Water-related issues are automatically assigned the highest priority, while other complaints are prioritized based on severity.

03

Authority Reviews Complaint

The complaint reaches the government dashboard, where authorities see complaints sorted by priority and begin taking action.

04

Email Notifications

The citizen receives an email after complaint submission containing the Complaint ID and another email once the complaint is marked as resolved.
